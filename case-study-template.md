## The calls

Three to six decisions, one line each, stated as decisions rather than
activities. "Rank by cohort, not by individual" is a call. "Built a ranking
system" is a task. If a line does not name something you chose over something
else, it does not belong here.

**Scope.** What you owned, and what partner teams owned.

**Reading the numbers.** Define any metric a reader outside the company would
not know. State the read window and any limit on how far the result generalises.

## The situation

What was true when you started. Short and factual, no scene setting.

## The constraint

The structural problem, not the schedule. A regulation, a dependency you did not
control, two metrics that could not move together. If there is no real
constraint, this is not a case study.

## The decision

The call, then the reasoning. Give the alternative you were choosing against.

## What I turned down

Two or three options you seriously considered and why each lost. Most portfolios
skip this. It is the part that shows judgement rather than outcome.

## What the measurement showed

Results in relative terms only. Include what went wrong; something did. Where a
conclusion is your reading rather than something the analysis stated, say so.

## What I would do differently

Something specific you now know that you did not then.
