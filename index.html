--- layout: home headline: Case studies from seven years of consumer product work. standfirst: I'm a senior product manager at Flutter Entertainment, working on growth, monetisation and platform across India, the UK and Ireland. Each case study sets out the constraint, the decision I took, the alternatives I considered, and what the measurement showed. ---

Scope

Category

I own a consumer category that has grown roughly 20x in monthly gross profit over three years, driven by repeat purchase rather than acquisition spend.

Platform

I set the vision for the multitenant platform that runs four consumer brands across three markets off one codebase, and own the admin console the brand teams operate it through.

Monetisation

After India banned real money gaming, I chose and sequenced the four models that replaced the revenue the ban removed.

Team

I manage four product managers and two product analysts, and own the quarterly roadmap, prioritisation and experiment design across the portfolio.
