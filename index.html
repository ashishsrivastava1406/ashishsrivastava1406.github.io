Product decisions, and the evidence behind them.

I'm a senior product manager working on consumer growth, monetisation and platform. These case studies are the calls I made, the alternatives I turned down, and what the numbers said afterwards, including the times they said I was wrong.

Where I've had the most effect

Category growth

Grew the consumer category I own roughly 20x in monthly gross profit over three years, on repeat purchase rather than acquisition spend.

Platform

Set the vision for the multitenant platform that runs four consumer brands across India, the UK and Ireland off one codebase, plus the admin console brand teams operate it through.

Monetisation

Rebuilt revenue from zero after India banned real money gaming, choosing and sequencing four replacement models under a live regulatory constraint.

Team

Manage four product managers and two product analysts, and own the quarterly roadmap, prioritisation and experiment design across the portfolio.

How to read these

Figures

Normalised throughout. Relative movement only, so multiples and point changes rather than absolute revenue, customer counts or per-brand retention rates. Screenshots have player handles, balances and promo codes redacted.

Attribution

Each case study states what I owned and what partner teams owned. Where a conclusion is my reading of the data rather than something the analysis stated outright, it says so.
