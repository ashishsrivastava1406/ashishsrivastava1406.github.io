## One category, five chapters

These case studies are not five separate projects. They are the anatomy of a
single consumer category — Rummy Tournaments — that grew roughly 20x in monthly
gross profit over three years, and then had its revenue model erased overnight
by a national regulatory ban.

Each one is a decision rather than a summary: what the constraint was, what I
chose, what I turned down, and what the numbers said afterwards — including
when they said I was wrong.

**[Tournament Lobby Revamp]({{ site.baseurl }}/tournament-lobby-revamp/)** ·
*discovery*
We built cohort personalisation into the lobby, then ran a three-path test that
could prove it unnecessary. It did. The bundle everybody clicked turned out to
be the one nobody played.

More chapters in progress: Tournament Ingress (entry), Recommendations
(ranking), Zombie Perception (perception), and F2P Revamp (monetisation, after
the ban).

## How to read these

Figures are normalised. Relative movement only — multiples, point changes,
directional language. No absolute revenue, customer counts or per-brand
retention rates from a current or former employer appear anywhere on this site.
Screenshots have player handles, balances and promo codes redacted.

Where a claim is my interpretation of the data rather than something the
analysis stated outright, it says so.
